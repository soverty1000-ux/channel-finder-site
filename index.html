An internal research tool for one small content team

Channel Finder is a tool that runs on the computers of a small team, led by An Gwangmin, that makes original Korean-language videos. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.

What it does

It helps the team decide which topics are worth making original videos about by looking at public YouTube statistics, for example topics that once drew large audiences but have not been covered recently. Results are shown only to the team in a dashboard on their own computers.

How it uses YouTube API Services

  • Public data. It reads video titles, view counts, publish dates and channel statistics with search.list, videos.list, channels.list and playlistItems.list. It reads public comments on a small number of selected videos with commentThreads.list to understand audience reactions.
  • The team's own channel. With the channel owner's Google sign-in (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly), it uploads the team's own videos and reads the team's own channel statistics. It does not access anyone else's account.
  • No redistribution. YouTube data is not sold, shared, published or redistributed.
  • No downloading. The tool does not download, cache or re-host YouTube video or audio content. Source footage for clips is received directly from the original creators.
  • Low usage. Each script has a hard cap on search calls per run, a daily counter stops runs at the cap, and recent results are reused instead of being requested again.

Data retention

Data is kept only in files on the team's own computers. Public YouTube data is deleted or refreshed within 30 calendar days. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
