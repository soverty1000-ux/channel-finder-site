Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Who we are

Channel Finder (WeMove) is a research tool used internally by An Gwangmin. It is not offered to the public. Contact: soverty@kakao.com.

YouTube API Services

Channel Finder uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy at http://www.google.com/policies/privacy.

Data we access

  • Public YouTube data: video titles, view counts, publish dates, channel names and channel statistics.
  • Public comments on a small number of selected videos, read to understand audience reactions to scenes.
  • The team's own channel, only with the channel owner's Google sign-in (OAuth scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly): to upload the team's own videos and read the team's own channel statistics, including audience retention, traffic sources, impressions and click-through rate from the YouTube Analytics and Reporting APIs. These reports are stored as received, without creating new metrics. We do not access any other person's Google or YouTube account.

How we use it

The data is shown only to the team in a dashboard on their own computers, to choose topics for original videos. We do not sell, share, publish or redistribute it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

  • Data is stored in files on the team's own computers. It is not stored on this website.
  • Public YouTube data, including comment text, is deleted or refreshed within 30 calendar days. Collection history files are trimmed automatically to the last 30 days.
  • The local tool keeps a few display preferences (for example favorites and region) in the browser's local storage on the team's computers.

Revoking access and deletion

The channel owner can revoke this tool's access to their Google account at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After access is revoked, data obtained through that access is deleted within 7 calendar days. If you want data related to you (for example a public comment you wrote) removed from our files, contact soverty@kakao.com and we will delete it within 7 calendar days.

This website

This website is a static page that does not use cookies or analytics. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs under its own privacy policy.

Children

Channel Finder is not directed to children and is not used by the public.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
